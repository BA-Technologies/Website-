Building and owning software businesses for the long term.

BA Technologies builds, acquires, and operates software businesses focused on solving meaningful problems and creating lasting customer value. Founded in Nigeria, we are building toward a portfolio of durable technology businesses serving customers across markets.

We believe lasting technology businesses are built through useful products, sound operations, and a long-term commitment to customers.

A different approach to building technology businesses.

BA Technologies is developing a model centered on acquiring promising software businesses, improving how they operate, and building new products where there is a clear opportunity to solve an important problem.

Our focus is on the fundamentals: understanding customers, allocating resources carefully, maintaining product quality, and building businesses that can create value over time.

BA Technologies Limited was incorporated in Nigeria on February 24, 2026. We are an early-stage company and do not yet own an operating portfolio.

Three complementary ways to build lasting value.

Acquire

We look for useful software businesses with real customers, established products, and opportunities for long-term growth.

  • Existing products and customer relationships.
  • Clear business fundamentals.
  • Opportunities for thoughtful improvement.

Operate and Improve

We work to make businesses more useful, efficient, reliable, and better positioned to serve their customers.

  • Product quality and usability.
  • Operational discipline.
  • Customer experience and retention.

Build

We develop new software products when we identify meaningful problems, underserved customers, and compelling opportunities.

  • Practical product development.
  • Clear customer needs.
  • Sustainable business models.

What guides our decisions.

Long-term ownership

We focus on building businesses that remain valuable to their customers over time.

Customer first

We prioritize practical problems, reliable products, and customer relationships built on trust.

Disciplined execution

Continuous improvement

Built a software business? Let's talk.

Building a software business takes time, persistence, and a deep understanding of customers.

BA Technologies is interested in connecting with founders and owners who have built useful software products and are considering the next chapter for their businesses.

We are particularly interested in established products with real users, clear customer value, and opportunities for continued development.

We approach every conversation with the understanding that each business has its own history, customers, and operating needs.

If you own a software business and are considering a sale, partnership, or long-term transition, we would welcome an initial conversation.

We cannot respond to every enquiry with an offer, and we do not publish acquisition prices, deal structures, or timelines.
